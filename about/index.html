About StudyAI Hub

StudyAI Hub started as a personal spreadsheet: a college student keeping track of which AI tools were actually worth using for coursework, and which were hype. It outgrew the spreadsheet.

This site has one job — tell a student, in under a minute, whether a tool is worth their time and whether it is really free. Everything else follows from that.

Why this site exists

Search "best AI tools for students" and you get lists assembled from press releases, padded to fifty entries, where half the "free" tools ask for a card before you can do anything useful. StudyAI Hub is the correction: a short list of tools that were actually opened, used and checked for limits, with the downsides written down next to the upsides.

How tools get listed

  • Every tool is opened and worked with before it gets a review here — not summarised from a marketing page.
  • Free-tier limits are found and written down honestly, because a free trial is not a free plan. Anything trial-only is labelled paid.
  • Dead, acquired or newly paywalled tools get removed. Listings are re-checked monthly.
  • Every listing carries a stated limitation. A tool with no downsides noted has not been used properly.

The full standard, including what "free" means on this site and how ratings are decided, is written out on the How we test page.

How we make money

This site may earn a commission if you sign up for a paid plan through some outbound links, at no extra cost to you. A commission never changes a rating, an ordering, or whether a tool is listed at all. Star ratings and rankings are not for sale, and if that ever changes this page will say so plainly before it happens.

Corrections and contact

Found a great tool we're missing? A price that changed? A broken link? Reach out through the contact page — the list improves because readers flag things. Corrections are made in place, and a tool that keeps failing re-checks is removed for good.
