Free Software Perks for Students in 2026 (Verified)

Premium software that is 100% free with a .edu email

GitHub Student Developer Pack

Free
Bundle · Verified students

The single best thing I signed up for with my .edu email. GitHub Copilot is completely free - normally $10 a month - plus cloud credits, free domains, and a pile of developer tools. If you write any code at all, verify your student status today. I tell every CS friend about this first.

JetBrains Student License

Free
Free subscription · Students + teachers

The entire JetBrains suite - IntelliJ, PyCharm, WebStorm, plus their AI assistant - free for students, renewable yearly. These are the professional tools that cost hundreds of dollars a year in the real world. My first week using PyCharm felt like an upgrade I didn't deserve.

Namecheap for Education

Free
Free domain · Verified students

Verified students get a free .me domain for a year - I used mine for my portfolio site and it looks way more professional than a free subdomain. Renewal isn't free after year one, so treat it as a starter domain. Cheap .coms too if you're building something real.

.TECH Domains for Students

Free
Free domain · Verified students

Got a free .tech domain through the student program for my side project blog - it reads more techie than .me for sure. One year free, then renewal costs money, so treat it as a starter, not a forever home. Fine for a portfolio or a project that might not live long anyway.

GitLab for Education

Free
Free subscription · Students + teachers

Students get the top Ultimate tier free - advanced CI/CD, security scanning, project management features that cost real money in the corporate world. If your course or side project touches DevOps at all, grab it. My group project's pipelines have never looked smoother.

Azure for Students

Free
Cloud credit · Verified students

$100 in Azure credit plus free-for-12-months services, and the killer part: no credit card required. As a broke student that's what sold me. You also get Azure OpenAI access at credit rates, which was genuinely useful for my AI coursework.

AWS Educate

Free
Cloud credit · Students + educators

Free AWS credits plus self-paced cloud labs and a badge system that looks decent on a resume. If your class touches cloud computing at all, sign up - the labs alone are worth it. I used it to learn the basics without touching my own money.

Google Cloud for Students

Free
Cloud credit · Students via faculty

The catch with Google Cloud credits: they go through faculty, not students. Find a professor enrolled in the program and your course can get credits plus free Qwiklabs courses. Handy if you want GCP's ML and data tooling for a project.

DigitalOcean Student Credits

Free
Cloud credit · GitHub Student Pack members

Part of the GitHub Student Pack - cloud credit to spin up droplets, which are simple VPS servers. If you want to host a side project on a real server without Azure's complexity, this is the friendliest option I've used. Clean dashboard, great docs.

MongoDB Atlas for Students

Free
Cloud credit · Verified students

If your database course uses MongoDB, the student program gets you Atlas cloud credit plus the free 512MB shared tier. I ran my capstone project's backend on it for a whole semester without paying. Perfect for a real production-ish cluster for school work.

Notion for Education

Free
Free subscription · Students + teachers

Notion Plus is free with a school email - unlimited uploads and version history for as long as you study. It replaced my planner, notes app, and to-do list with one thing. The template gallery alone is worth the signup.

Perplexity Pro for Students

Free
Free subscription · Verified students

Perplexity runs free Pro for verified students from time to time - same models as paying users, with the cited answers included. I claimed it with my .edu email and it's been my research sidekick since. Check their education page while the offer's active.

Figma for Education

Free
Free subscription · Students + teachers

Figma's professional tier is free for students - unlimited projects, team libraries, and the full plugin ecosystem including AI plugins. Even my non-designer friends end up using it for group project mockups. The industry standard, free while you're in school - take it.

Canva for Education

Free
Free subscription · Teachers + K-12 students

K-12 teachers and students get full Canva Pro free - every premium template, stock image, and AI tool unlocked. College students should check if their school has a campus license. Even without it, the free tier is generous enough for most student work.

Adobe Creative Cloud Student

Discount
Discount · Verified students

Adobe's student plan is roughly 65% off the full Creative Cloud - Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects. It's not free like the others here, but for design and media students it's the best price you'll ever get on industry-standard tools. I split it with a classmate.

Autodesk Education

Free
Free subscription · Students + educators

Autodesk opens its whole catalog - AutoCAD, Maya, 3ds Max, Fusion 360, Revit - to students for free. For engineering, architecture, and 3D/animation students, this is the single most valuable free perk that exists. Fusion 360 alone costs thousands commercially.

Tableau for Students

Free
Free subscription · Verified students

If you're in any data analytics course, get Tableau Desktop Pro free for a year with a student license - the same license costs real money in the corporate world. Learning it now is a solid resume move if data roles are your target. I put it on my resume the same week.

LinkedIn Learning (via campus)

Free
Free subscription · Students via university

Most universities buy a campus license - check your library portal. The course library is huge and the certificates push straight to your LinkedIn profile, which recruiters actually see. I knocked out a few Excel and public speaking courses between semesters.

Coursera for Campus

Free
Free subscription · Students via university

If your university participates, you get free access to a big catalog of courses and guided projects - Google, IBM, and university courses with real certificates. I took a Google course that actually meant something on my resume. Ask your school if they're enrolled.

O'Reilly Learning (via campus)

Free
Free subscription · Students via university

If your university library subscribes, this is a goldmine - every tech book they've published, video courses, and interactive coding sandboxes. For CS students especially: read the same books the pros use, free. I stopped buying coding books entirely.

DataCamp for Students

Free
Free subscription · Verified students

Verified students get free premium access for a semester - the full catalog of Python, R, SQL, and data science courses with in-browser coding. If you want data skills on your resume without paying for a bootcamp, this is the most hands-on option here.

Apple Education Pricing

Discount
Discount · Verified students + parents + teachers

Verified students get discounted Mac, iPad, and accessories - usually $100-200 off. If you were buying a Mac anyway, it's free money. The Pro Apps Bundle (Final Cut, Logic) is also deeply discounted, which is what sold me.

Spotify Premium Student

Discount
Discount · Verified students

Half price plus Hulu (with ads) and SHOWTIME bundled in - genuinely the best streaming value a student can get. You re-verify yearly and it caps at four years, but for ad-free music while studying it can't be beaten. I signed up the day I got my .edu email.

YouTube Premium Student

Discount
Discount · Verified students

About 40% off regular price, removes all ads, enables background play, and throws in YouTube Music. For anyone who learns from YouTube - which is most of us - the ad-free plus background play combo during study sessions is worth the few dollars a month.

Amazon Prime Student

Discount
Discount · Verified students

Six months of Prime completely free, then half price for up to four years. The free two-day shipping alone justified it for my textbook orders, and Prime Video is a legit bonus. The trial is the longest free window of any perk here - claim it early in the semester.

UNiDAYS

Free
Bundle · Verified students

Not one perk - it's the key that unlocks hundreds of student discounts across Apple, Nike, Adidas, Samsung, and more. Sign up once with your .edu email and you're verified across most student-discount programs on the web. I've saved more than I can count on clothes alone.

Student Beans

Free
Bundle · Verified students

UNiDAYS' main competitor - another free aggregator of student discounts at hundreds of brands. The two overlap but each has exclusive partners, so it's worth having both. Student Beans is particularly strong in the UK and EU.
