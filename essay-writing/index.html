Best AI Writing Tools for Students in 2026

Draft, paraphrase, polish and grammar-check your assignments

Grammarly

Freemium
Final essay polish

I installed the browser extension freshman week and forgot about it until I wrote my first essay without it. It catches typos, passive voice, and tone issues everywhere - docs, emails, even messages to professors. I only paid for Premium during thesis season, and the student discount makes it like 40% cheaper. Worth every cent for that one month.

QuillBot

Freemium
Paraphrasing

My go-to for the classic problem: I know what I want to say but it comes out clunky. Paste it in, get a cleaner version. Also a lifesaver for condensing long readings into something I can actually skim before class. Fair warning - it rewrites but doesn't explain, so use it on your own draft, not to pretend you read the chapter.

ChatGPT (Writing Workflows)

Freemium
Outlines & first drafts

The way I actually use ChatGPT for essays: not to write them, but to argue with it. I ask for thesis options, outline structures, counterarguments to my own points - then I write the paper myself. Most schools now ask you to disclose AI use, so check your syllabus first. Treat it as the friend who brainstorms with you, not the one who writes your essay.

LanguageTool

Freemium
Grammar check without Grammarly's nagging

LanguageTool is what I recommend to every ESL friend who finds Grammarly's suggestions confusing. It supports 30+ languages, so my roommate checks her Spanish essays and I check my English papers in the same tool. The free version catches spelling, basic grammar and punctuation. It nags less than Grammarly, which I mean as a compliment.

Hemingway Editor

Free
Cutting bloated sentences

Paste your essay into Hemingway and watch it light up like a crime scene - yellow for rambling sentences, red for impossible ones, purple for filler words. My professor said my writing 'improved dramatically' the semester I ran every draft through it. It doesn't check grammar; it checks whether a tired reader can survive your prose.

ProWritingAid

Freemium
Deep-dive essay analysis

ProWritingAid is Grammarly for people who want a lecture, not a correction. Its 20+ reports told me my essay was 40% adverbs and that I started six paragraphs with 'However.' The free version only checks 500 words at a time, so I edit section by section - annoying, but it forces me to actually read the feedback.

Scribbr Citation Generator

Free
APA, MLA, Chicago citations

I finished a 15-source bibliography in ten minutes with Scribbr - paste a URL or DOI, pick your style, copy the citation. No account, no paywall, no 'upgrade to export.' It was the difference between me sleeping before a deadline and manually formatting MLA hanging indents at 4am. My entire friend group switched after I showed them.
