4 Best AI Presentation Makers (2026, Tested)

Class decks and project pitches in minutes, not all-nighters

Gamma

Freemium
Slides in 5 minutes

The 'PowerPoint takes three hours, Gamma takes five minutes' tool, and honestly it's not an exaggeration. Paste in a doc or a prompt and you get a class-ready deck with layout done. The free 400 credits cover four or five presentations - I save them for the ones actually worth something.

Canva

Freemium
Design control

The safe pick when I need something to look decent fast - slides, posters, infographics, even my resume. Less automatic than Gamma, but you keep full control of the design, and the AI assistant helps when I'm stuck. There's a template for literally everything.

Beautiful.ai

Freemium
Auto-designed slides

Beautiful.ai treats you like you have zero design sense, because I do. Add content and it rearranges everything to stay balanced - no more slides with one sad bullet point floating in a corner. The free trial is limited, but it saved my group project when the design-inclined member dropped the class a week before presenting.

Slidesgo

Freemium
Free slide templates that don't look like 2007

Slidesgo is where I go when the deadline is tomorrow and the deck needs to not look like every other presentation in class. Free templates for Google Slides and PowerPoint, organized by subject - the education section is huge. You do have to leave the attribution slide or sign up, and the free picks rotate monthly, which is mildly annoying.
